Why two different figures? Istat points out that part of the change depends on the ageing of the population. That is why, next to the observed figure, it publishes the one net of the demographic component.

Three checks before commenting on a figure

  1. Is it monthly or yearly? The comparison with the previous month and the one with the same month a year earlier can tell different stories.
  2. Is it seasonally adjusted? Istat's monthly figures are corrected for the effect of the seasons.
  3. Has it been revised? Istat revises the data every month: the value of a month can change in the next release.
