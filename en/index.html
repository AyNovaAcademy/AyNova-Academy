MILAN PRISON
The rose worth more than any certificate
Click to read ↓

I went into Milan prison to train inmates. I didn't know what to expect. I didn't know whether I would make a difference. At the end of the course they gave me a rose, handmade by them. In that moment I understood why I do this work. Not for titles. Not for money. For that moment. Because real training doesn't transfer skills. It transfers hope.

THE YOUNG MAN WITH A LEARNING DISORDER
"Dr. Sala, can I give a hand?"

The training body had told me to treat him differently. I didn't. To me he was like everyone else, with the same dignity and the same trust. He felt at ease and showed a talent nobody expected.
